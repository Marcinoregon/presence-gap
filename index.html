The Challenge

The Document Is Perfect.
The Human Being Is Not.

"A professional enters an interview room with a flawless AI-generated resume and a cover letter refined through multiple rounds of machine-assisted editing. When the interviewer asks a question that requires genuine self-knowledge or authentic presence, the disconnect becomes visible. The candidate knows what to say. They do not know who they are when they say it."

— The Presence Gap, v5a
▲

AI Automates the Output

Generative AI can now write your resume, cover letter, and interview prep in seconds — bypassing the reflective work through which career identity is actually built.

▼

The Developmental Cost Is Hidden

The output looks polished. But the person hasn't done the inner work. AI removes the developmental scaffolding while preserving the result.

The Gap Widens

The Framework

Three Dimensions of Disconnection

Each dimension maps 1:1 to an established career development theory. Each has distinct manifestations — and distinct interventions.

01
Career Construction Theory

Disconnection from Self

A disruption in identity clarity, values alignment, and inhabitable career narrative. When AI generates your professional story, you arrive at career situations with outputs you cannot inhabit.

EHC Strain: Authentic Presence
02
Relational-Cultural Theory

Disconnection from Others

More contact, less connection. The co-presence freeze — delayed responses, physical stillness, conversational difficulty — is a protective adaptation, not a skill deficit.

EHC Strain: Relational Depth
03
Psychology of Working Theory

Disconnection from Purpose

Executing tasks disconnected from why they matter. Algorithmic optimization orients professionals toward externally defined metrics rather than internally held values.

EHC Strain: Purposive Engagement
CHRONIC PRESENCE GAP → Sustained, high-level disconnection across all three dimensions places the Essential Human Core under strain.
What Is at Stake

The Essential Human Core

The constellation of distinctively human capacities that AI cannot replicate — and that a chronic Presence Gap places under sustained strain.

◕

Authentic Presence

The capacity for full, undivided engagement with the immediate relational environment. AI can simulate attentiveness — it cannot be present.

◼

Relational Depth

Professional relationships characterized by mutual vulnerability, sustained care, and trust built through consistent authentic presence over time.

◆

Purposive Engagement

Action coherently connected to a meaningful sense of why — reflecting actual values and sustaining motivation through difficulty. AI can optimize toward objectives; it cannot generate purpose.

Formal Definition

Presence Gap: An integrative construct describing the discrepancy between a person's inner capacity and desire for connection and their lived participation in real career situations, amplified by digital overload — including but not limited to AI tools — and deepened when generative AI displaces the reflective and values-clarifying processes through which career identity, relational capacity, and purpose are ordinarily built.

Practitioner Applications

The Presence Gap Toolkit

Four evidence-informed tools for career counselors — each targeting a specific dimension, restoring the developmental friction that AI efficiency removes.

Tool 01 Self-disconnection

Values Audit Protocol

The counselor interrogates AI-generated career materials with the client present — demanding narrative accountability for every substantive claim. "Tell me a specific story of when you actually embodied that."

Grounded in Career Construction Theory
Tool 02 Narrative recovery

Analog Artifact Exercise

Clients describe a career achievement using only physical objects or hand drawings — no devices. The counselor listens for the raw, emotionally textured narrative AI could never generate.

Grounded in Career Construction Theory
Tool 03 Other-disconnection

Relational Stakeholder Map

Clients map only relationships organized by mutual vulnerability — not utility. Reveals the real relational architecture beneath LinkedIn connection counts.

Grounded in Relational-Cultural Theory
Tool 04 AI use protocol

AI as Translation Engine

A 5-step framework: Excavate → Select → Translate → Review → Inhabit. AI enters only at Step 3 — after the person has already generated the authentic content.

CCT + Psychology of Working Theory
Self-Assessment

Where Is Your Presence Gap?

Answer three questions honestly. This is not a clinical instrument — it is a reflective prompt to locate your disconnection.

Dimension 1 — Self

Could you narrate, in a live conversation, the real story behind every claim in your current resume or professional bio?

Dimension 2 — Others

How many people in your professional life have seen you fail — and the relationship survived?

Dimension 3 — Purpose

When you finish a workday, can you articulate — in your own words — why the work you did today mattered?

Theoretical Foundations

  • Career Construction Theory (Savickas, 2013) — Self-disconnection
  • Relational-Cultural Theory (Miller & Stiver, 1997) — Other-disconnection
  • Psychology of Working Theory (Blustein, 2011) — Purpose-disconnection
  • SCCT (Lent, Brown & Hackett, 1994) — Mechanism lens

Interim Psychometric Proxies

  • CAAS — Career Adapt-Abilities Scale (self-disconnection)
  • RHI — Relational Health Indices (other-disconnection)
  • MLQ / DWS — Meaning in Life / Decent Work Scale (purpose)
  • CDSE-SF — Career Decision-Making Self-Efficacy (SCCT mechanism)

Two Testable Propositions

P1: Perceived employability correlates more strongly with authentic relational presence than with technical proficiency as AI adoption increases.

P2: The Presence Gap negatively moderates the relationship between technical competence and organizational career outcomes via SCCT self-efficacy deprivation.
